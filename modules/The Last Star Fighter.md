## History:  [The Last Star Fighter](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4R2UFlSc4mI)
- Major step in computer graphics
- Extensive (30 min) of visual effects
- What is impressive about the visual effects?
- What doesn't look realistic about the effects? Why?
- Does anything bend or move in the effects?
  - No. This is a great example of rigid body animation--animation where the objects change their root transforms, but the mesh itself doesn't change
  - This matches most of the visual effects in Star Wars, where the space craft are rigid models.
